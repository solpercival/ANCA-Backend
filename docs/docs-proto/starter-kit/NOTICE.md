# Usage & confidentiality

These materials — the documentation, sample alarms, brand assets, and mock API in this
starter kit — are provided by **ANCA Motion** to University of Melbourne students **solely for
the COMP30022 IT Project (ANCA Mate), Semester 2, 2026**.

Please:

- **Keep them private.** Do not post the ANCA-provided materials in public repositories, or
  publish them anywhere publicly accessible. Use **private** GitHub/GitLab repositories for your
  project work.
- **Use them only for this project.** They're a starting point for your capstone, not for any
  other purpose.
- **Treat the documentation as confidential and work-in-progress.** Some content is draft and
  may contain errors.

Intellectual property and confidentiality for this project are handled through the University's
standard capstone process. If anything here is unclear, please check with your workshop
supervisor (Kian) or the ANCA Motion client contact (Tim Comport) before sharing.

Thanks — we're glad to share this with you and looking forward to what you build.
